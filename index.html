1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children. This App is designed for children and complies with Google Play Families Policy.

2. No Data Collected

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. This includes:

  • Names, emails, or contact details

No account or login is required to use this App.

3. Offline App

This App works completely offline. No data is ever sent to any server.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

This App is specifically designed for young children learning the Bangla alphabet. We take children's privacy very seriously.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • No personal information is required to use this App.
  • This App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • This App complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • This App complies with Google Play Families Policy.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at ratiburrahaman@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

5. Advertising

This App shows ads through Google AdMob. To protect children, we have configured AdMob with the following settings:

  • Child-directed treatment is enabled.
  • Maximum ad content rating is set to G (General Audiences) — suitable for all ages.
  • Only family-friendly, non-personalized ads are served.

Google AdMob may collect limited device information to display ads. This is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Free App

This App is completely free with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. It is supported by AdMob advertisements.

7. Third-Party Services

This App only uses Google AdMob for advertising. No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or social media integrations are used.
